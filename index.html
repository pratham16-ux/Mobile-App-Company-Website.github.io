The right technology for your product

We pick tools for your goals, not the other way round.

Native iOS with Swift

Best when you need the smoothest animations, deep Apple integrations or the newest iOS features on day one.

  • SwiftUI and UIKit
  • Apple Pay, HealthKit and widgets
  • TestFlight beta distribution

Native Android with Kotlin

Best for apps that rely on device hardware, background work or wide Android device coverage.

  • Jetpack Compose
  • Google Pay and Play Billing
  • Tested across low-end to flagship phones

Flutter

One codebase for iOS and Android with a custom, pixel-perfect interface and fast iteration.

  • Single team, single release cycle
  • Rich animation support
  • Lower cost for the first version

React Native

A strong fit when your team already works in JavaScript or TypeScript and shares logic with a web app.

  • Shared code with your web product
  • Over-the-air updates
  • Large library ecosystem
